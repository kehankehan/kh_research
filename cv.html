Research profile

I am a cognitive scientist investigating how embodied experience becomes organised—from real-time perception and action to emotion concepts expressed through language—and whether artificial systems recover the same organisation. I examine this process across development, expertise, languages and cultures, combining neurophysiology, behavioural experiments, eye tracking and wearable sensing with computational analyses of representational geometry.
